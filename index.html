Solntse

animated album-cover artwork — a liquid ink sun with editable type

Draw a sun and set type around it: every slider reshapes the ink live, then export a still as SVG or PNG, or the animation as GIF or video. After the sleeve of Звезда по имени Солнце (“A Star Called the Sun”), the last album KINO released before Viktor Tsoi died, out 29 August 1989. Nothing is traced from it — the sun is generated from scratch on every frame, and the defaults simply land near that cover. Reset to cover returns them.
