Solntse

An animated album-cover artwork: a liquid ink sun around a black circle, with editable heading and title text above and below it.

A tribute to the sleeve of Звезда по имени Солнце (“A Star Called the Sun”) — the sixth and final studio album KINO released before Viktor Tsoi died, out 29 August 1989. Nothing here is traced from that cover: the sun is generated from scratch on every frame. The defaults — yellow ink, black ground, КИНО above, the title below in red — are just tuned to land near it, and Reset to cover puts them back.
